Challenge 3 – Cryptography

Challenge Description

Sensitive information is sometimes hidden using encoding or simple encryption techniques. Security testers often need to decode or decrypt messages to retrieve hidden data. In this challenge, you will decode different types of encoded text to find flags.

Objectives

  • Easy: Decode a Base64 encoded string.
  • Medium: Decrypt a Caesar cipher message.
  • Hard: Reverse and decode a multi-encoded string.

Easy - Base64

Decode this Base64 string:

SFB7YmFzZTY0X2Vhc3l9

Medium - Caesar Cipher

Decrypt this text (Shift 3):

KS{fdhvdu_flskhu}

Hard - Reverse + Base64

Reverse the string, then decode Base64:

9Rmchh2XvRHc5J3Y7BFS
