Challenge 4 – Cookies

Challenge Description

Web applications store user data and permissions inside browser cookies. If cookies are not properly protected, attackers may read or modify them to gain unauthorized access. Inspect and manipulate cookies to find hidden flags.

Objectives

  • Easy: Inspect cookies to find the flag.
  • Medium: Decode an encoded cookie value.
  • Hard: Modify the cookie role to gain admin access.

Easy

The flag is stored in cookies.

Medium

The cookie value is encoded.

Hard

Change your cookie role to admin.
